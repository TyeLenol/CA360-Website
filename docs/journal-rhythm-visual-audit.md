# Journal Rhythm Refinement Audit

## First live verification

The revised start rail now measures approximately 780px at the tested desktop viewport, down from the previous roughly 920px range implied by the larger padding and card minimums. The three entry cards remain readable and visually substantial, but they no longer feel like a second full hero chapter.

The featured story remains visually dominant immediately below the start rail. Its image/text split, large serif title, metadata, venue, author, and preview CTA are still intact. The feature did not need to be reduced; the surrounding start rail now hands off to it more directly.

The route still reads as a premium editorial experience, but the first-time flow has less vertical drag before the lead story becomes visible.

## Featured-story verification

The feature still renders as the dominant chapter: the image occupies the larger left column, the title remains large and legible, and the metadata, excerpt, venue, author, and preview CTA remain intact. The feature reads as a deliberate editorial anchor rather than an oversized mistake.

The main improvement is the handoff: the compacted start rail ends sooner, and the feature begins with less dead space and less reveal choreography around it.
